EQ vs empathy

Related, not identical.

Empathy helps you understand another person's inner world. EQ includes empathy, then adds self-awareness, self-control, values, and repair.

Emotional intelligence

The whole skill set

EQ asks: What am I feeling? What is it making me want to do? What response would be clear, kind, and effective?

Self-awareness Regulation Social skill Repair
Empathy

The people-reading skill

Empathy asks: What might they be thinking, feeling, needing, protecting, or hoping for, and how can I respond with clarity and care?
