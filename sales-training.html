Skill by repetition

Habits of a great salesperson

Sales confidence comes from a tight feedback loop: do a little selling, observe what happened, improve one thing, repeat.

01

Talk to prospects daily

Real conversations build instincts faster than theory. Make outreach a small daily rep.

02

Ask more than you explain

Diagnose first. The buyer should feel understood before they feel pitched.

03

Listen for pain

Notice frustration, urgency, money loss, time loss, fear, desire, and status signals.

04

Track objections

Keep a running list of price, trust, timing, and need objections. Prepare calm answers.

05

Follow up consistently

Most people do not buy immediately. Follow up with context, usefulness, and respect.

06

Practice your offer line

I help [person] get [result] without [pain].

07

Review lost deals

Ask where you lost trust, urgency, clarity, timing, or fit. Then adjust the next call.

08

Build proof

Collect testimonials, screenshots, before-after examples, case studies, and results.

09

Roleplay discomfort

Practice asking for money, hearing no, and making direct closes until they feel normal.

Calm courage

How to overcome shyness when asking

Shyness softens when you stop seeing the ask as taking. A respectful ask is an invitation, and the other person is free to choose.

The no-pressure frame

"I'm working on something that may help with [problem]. Would you be open to taking a look?"

Give context, make the ask, and leave space for a no. That is confident, not pushy.

Use soft, clean asks

  • Would you be open to checking this out?
  • Is this something you'd like help with?
  • Would it make sense to move forward?
  • Could I ask a small favor?

Tiny daily asks

  • Can I get your opinion?
  • Could you introduce me?
  • Would you mind sharing this?
  • Can we set a time?
Old thought

"I am bothering them."

Better thought

"I am offering a useful option and letting them decide."

Practice rule

Your job is to ask respectfully. Their job is to decide.

Question ladder

SPIN selling method

SPIN helps you sell by understanding the buyer before pitching. It is useful for services, B2B offers, software, marketing, and anything where trust matters.

S

Situation

Understand the current state. Use these lightly.

  • How are you currently getting customers?
  • What tools are you using now?
  • How do you handle this process today?
P

Problem

Find pain points and friction.

  • What is not working well?
  • Where do you lose the most time?
  • What makes this difficult?
I

Implication

Help the buyer feel the cost of doing nothing.

  • What happens if this continues?
  • How much time or money does that cost?
  • Does this affect growth or customer trust?
N

Need-payoff

Let the buyer describe the value of the solution.

  • Would it help if this were automatic?
  • How valuable would saving 10 hours a week be?
  • If this were fixed, what would change?

Example

Website sale to a small business

  1. Situation: How do customers currently find you online?
  2. Problem: Do people ever ask whether your business is trustworthy before buying?
  3. Implication: If your site looks outdated, do some customers leave before contacting you?
  4. Need-payoff: If your site looked professional and brought inquiries to WhatsApp, would that help?

Choose the right tool

SPIN vs other sales methods

SPIN is excellent for trust-building conversations, but it works best when paired with clear copy, proof, follow-up, and a direct close.

MethodBest forStrengthWatch out
SPINConsultative sales, B2B, servicesBuilds trust through questionsCan feel slow when buyer wants quick pricing
AIDAAds, landing pages, simple pitchesGreat for grabbing attentionLess useful for complex conversations
ChallengerHigh-value B2B dealsTeaches the buyer something newCan feel pushy if done without empathy
Solution SellingServices and softwareStrong focus on solving painLess structured than SPIN
SandlerRelationship-led salesGood for qualification and pressure-free sellingTakes practice to sound natural
MEDDICEnterprise salesStrong deal qualificationToo heavy for small sales
BANTQuick lead qualificationSimple and fastCan miss emotional or strategic pain

Best combo for practice

SPIN for conversations, AIDA for copy, and a simple close at the end.
