Constructive influence

Persuasion begins as service.

The move is simple but rare: understand what the other person values, then present your request as a respectful path toward mutual benefit.

1

Make them feel important

Not flattered. Genuinely seen. Name their stake, pressure, role, or standard.

2

Create value first

Give information, options, relief, clarity, or credit before asking for action.

3

Invite, do not corner

People defend against pressure. They cooperate when they still feel free.

Words to use

Gentle persuasion scripts.

Open

"I want this to work for both of us. Can I share one option?"

Choice

"Would A or B be easier from your side?"

Value

"This helps you by reducing rework and giving you a cleaner decision."

Close

"What would make this a yes for you?"

Resistance

Do not push the wall. Find the door.

Fear

Ask: "What risk worries you most?"

Confusion

Ask: "What would make this clearer?"

Status

Ask: "How can we do this without making anyone look bad?"

Trust

Repair failed persuasion.

If you pushed too hard

"I came in too strong. Let me step back and understand your concern."

If they felt unheard

"I missed what mattered to you. What should I understand first?"
