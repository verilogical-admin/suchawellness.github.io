Toolkit

Mental models reduce blind spots.

A model is a thinking lens. The goal is not to sound clever; it is to see the decision from enough angles that fewer obvious errors survive.

I

Inversion

Instead of asking how to succeed, ask how to guarantee failure and avoid those moves.

2

Second order

Look past the first result into the next consequence, reaction, and habit it creates.

$

Opportunity cost

Every yes spends time, attention, money, reputation, or emotional energy.

%

Probabilities

Replace certainty theater with ranges, confidence levels, and update points.

Inversion

Ask the uncomfortable opposite.

Forward question

"How do I make this go well?"

Inverted question

"What would make this obviously fail, and what can I remove now?"

1. List failure paths. Misaligned incentives, unclear owner, rushed timeline, ignored downside.
2. Remove the easiest failure. Clarify, slow down, precommit, test, or ask the missing question.
3. Keep one tripwire. Decide in advance what signal means you should pause or change course.

Systems

Behavior follows incentives and feedback loops.

Loop

Reinforcing loop

Success creates more resources, which creates more success. Useful when healthy, dangerous when addictive.

Delay

Delayed feedback

Some choices feel good now and invoice you later. Some feel hard now and pay later.

Incentive

Incentive map

Ask what behavior the system rewards, not what it says it values.
