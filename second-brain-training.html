Core loop

Save less. Use more.

A second brain is not a hoarding closet for notes. It is a working system that helps you notice what matters and turn it into action.

C

Capture

Collect only ideas that surprise you, solve a problem, support a project, or feel emotionally sticky.

O

Organize

File notes where they will be used, not where they theoretically belong.

D

Distill

Bold the point, summarize the note, and make future-you able to understand it fast.

E

Express

Turn saved knowledge into a memo, decision, lesson, plan, script, or shipped artifact.

Progressive summarization

Make notes easier to reuse each time you touch them.

1

Raw capture

Save the idea with source and context. Do not polish yet.

2

Bold the signal

Highlight the one or two lines that would matter in a future project.

3

Write a handle

Add a plain-language summary: "Use this when..." or "This proves..."
