Reflection Prompts

Make the video useful.

1

What is the main idea?

Write one sentence in plain language. If it cannot be summarized, it may need more checking.

2

What applies to me?

Separate general information from your actual situation, history, risks, and current supports.

3

What should I verify?

Bring medical, legal, safety, or high-stakes questions to qualified professionals instead of relying on a video.
